Analytical interpretation

Read the advisory as a comparison, not a single target

The platform is organized around agronomically meaningful comparators.

Yield

Predicted grain yield is shown with its prediction interval where available, so uncertainty remains visible.

Nitrogen use efficiency

AE-N is used where a valid zero-N reference exists; PFP-N supports comparisons where that reference is not consistently available.

Potential mineral-N reduction

Lower-N alternatives are expressed relative to their comparator. This is a scenario estimate, not an observed fertilizer loss.

Spatial extrapolation

Spatial products extend trial evidence cautiously within the study domain. Modelled soil covariates do not replace measured trial-site soils.
