NSAF ALL SCRIPTS — FIXED COMPARATIVE MAP SCALE

Cartographic rule applied throughout:
- Comparable map panels in the same figure use one common geographic extent.
- The common extent is derived from the union of represented study districts in that figure/stage.
- Individual panels do not auto-zoom by year, nutrient, treatment or strategy.
- EPSG:4326 is retained; longitude/latitude remain in degrees.
- Ward boundaries are light grey; district boundaries are stronger.
- District labels are boundary-derived callouts.
- Site points are above all boundary layers.
- Comparable yield panels share one yield colour scale.
- Response maps remain centered at zero: negative red, zero yellow, positive green.
- AE palettes remain sequential light-to-dark: AE-N YlGnBu, AE-P YlOrBr, AE-K PuRd.

All analytical definitions, stage names and agronomic terminology from the final 0a-1g and post-1g packages are unchanged.
