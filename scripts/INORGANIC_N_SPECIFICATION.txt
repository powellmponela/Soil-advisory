INORGANIC-N CONVENTION

For all N-management strategies, including FYM + mineral N:

AE-N of inorganic N =
    (Y_treatment - Y_0PK) * 1000 / N_inorganic

Reference:
    0PK = N0-P60-K40

For FYM + N60:
    denominator = 60 kg inorganic N ha-1
    FYM-N is not added to the denominator.

Advisory metrics:
    AE-N gain (%) = 100 * (AE-N_strategy - AE-N_GR) / AE-N_GR
    Inorganic-N reduction (%) = 100 * (120 - N_inorganic_strategy) / 120
    Yield change (%) = 100 * (Y_strategy - Y_GR) / Y_GR
